All tools by hiring demand

Ranked by the number of distinct companies whose job postings name the tool, from the API Evangelist demand corpus. This is a demand signal — evidence of hiring, not of deployment — and counts are only comparable within a single quarter.

Precision is a separate axis: how trustworthy the tool's name is as a search term in a job-postings corpus, independent of how many companies said it. A name that is also an ordinary word scores low however large its count. Filter on both — low precision with a high count is where a number is least safe to quote.
